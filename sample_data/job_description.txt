Title: Senior Backend Engineer - Platform Team

We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to help build and scale
our core platform services.

Requirements:
- 5+ years professional software engineering experience
- Strong Python or Node.js backend experience
- Experience designing REST APIs and working with relational databases
  (PostgreSQL or MySQL)
- Experience with distributed systems concepts: caching, queues,
  eventual consistency
- Comfortable working with Docker and at least one major cloud
  provider (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Bonus: experience with LLM/agent frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph)
  or the Model Context Protocol
- Bonus: experience mentoring junior engineers or leading small
  technical initiatives

We value engineers who can operate independently, write clean and
well-tested code, and communicate tradeoffs clearly.
